NIS2 / ZoKB — who does what

An offline orientation map under zákon č. 264/2025 Sb. Not legal advice.

Cybersecurity under the new Act is not one person's job. It is a chain of roles, each with a clear part. Here is the plain-language version.

Top management (board / statutory body)

Accountable — and cannot delegate the accountability. Management approves the risk approach, provides resources, and answers for the outcome. Under NIS2 this responsibility sits explicitly at the top.

Cybersecurity Manager (MKB / CISO)

Required in the higher regime. Owns the security management system day to day: risk analysis, controls, the incident process, documentation. The person who turns the Act into actual practice — and who talks to NÚKIB.

Responsible contact person

Every regulated entity designates someone to communicate with NÚKIB and to handle registration and reporting. In smaller organisations this may be the same person as the manager.

IT / operations

Implements the technical measures: multi-factor authentication, network segmentation, encryption, logging, patching, backups — and the detection that makes incident reporting possible in the first place.

Every employee

The largest attack surface and the first sensor. Recognises and reports suspicious activity, follows access and clear-screen practice, completes awareness training. Most incidents begin — and can end — here.

The reporting reflex

When something looks wrong, it goes to the responsible person → NÚKIB portal, fast. Reporting promptly is meant to bring registration and help, not an automatic inspection. Keep the numbers handy (see nukib-contacts.vcf).
