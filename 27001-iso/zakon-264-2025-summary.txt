zákon č. 264/2025 Sb. — o kybernetické bezpečnosti (ZoKB)
The new Czech Cybersecurity Act — a one-page plain summary
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Not legal advice. Public information, assembled for orientation.
Verify against the official sources at the bottom.

WHAT IT IS
  The Czech transposition of the EU NIS2 Directive (2022/2555).
  Effective 1 November 2025. It replaces the previous Act and
  dramatically widens who is regulated — by official estimates,
  more than 6,000 organisations across many sectors.

THE FIRST STEP: SELF-IDENTIFICATION
  The law does not send you a letter. You are expected to check
  yourself whether you are in scope, and if so, in which of two
  regimes:
    - HIGHER  ("essential" / vyšší režim)  — stricter duties
    - LOWER   ("important" / nižší režim)   — lighter duties
  NÚKIB publishes a public guide and a self-identification
  calculator on its portal to help you decide.

KEY DEADLINE
  If you are regulated, you must report that you provide a regulated
  service to NÚKIB — via the NÚKIB portal — within 60 days of the
  Act taking effect (i.e. counted from 1 November 2025; verify the
  exact date for your case). Miss nothing: put it on a calendar.

CORE OBLIGATIONS (high level)
  - Designate a person responsible for communication with NÚKIB.
  - In the higher regime, appoint a Cybersecurity Manager.
  - Perform a risk analysis.
  - Run an information-security management system (ISMS).
  - Set up detection AND reporting of incidents.
  - Implement technical measures: multi-factor authentication,
    network segmentation, encryption, logging, and more.
  - Keep documentation.

INCIDENT REPORTING
  Primarily through the NÚKIB portal. Reporting promptly is meant to
  get you registration, analysis, and offered help — not, in the
  first instance, an inspection or a fine. Contacts are in the
  accompanying nukib-contacts.vcf.

SANCTIONS (worst case)
  Higher regime:  up to 250,000,000 CZK or 2 %   of net global turnover.
  Lower regime:   up to 175,000,000 CZK or 1.4 % of net global turnover.
