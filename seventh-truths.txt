## Opening

This is the community canon — the part of 0-click I did not write. Six
author truths set the rhythm (below); the seventh, and every one after
it, is open. Anyone may write one and earn its place by pull request: I
close the PR without merging and append the accepted line here by hand.
Recognition, not absorption — your truth stays yours, credited, not
swallowed into mine.

The anthology caps at 42 per node. When it fills, this canon is closed
and the loop carries forward through the next node. A truth here is not a
slogan; it is something true about attention, machines, or fear, said in
one clean breath.
